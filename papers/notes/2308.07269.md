# 2308.07269 — EasyEdit: An Easy-to-use Knowledge Editing Framework for LLMs (Wang, Zhang, Tian, et al.; ACL 2024 demo)
Skimmed: abstract, framework description, Tables 1–2. Code: https://github.com/zjunlp/EasyEdit (docs: https://zjunlp.gitbook.io/easyedit; HF demo space zjunlp/EasyEdit).

- **Framework.** Modular pipeline with an Editor (`BaseEditor.edit(prompts, target_new, ground_truth, rephrase_prompts, locality_inputs, portability_inputs)`), Method (`apply_to_model`), Hparams (YAML per method and model), Evaluate, and Trainer (for MEND/SERAC hypernetworks and classifiers). Supported settings: single-instance, batch, sequential and multimodal editing.
- **Supported models:** T5, GPT-2, GPT-J, LLaMA/LLaMA-2/3, Mistral, Qwen, and others. Later versions add Gemma, Phi and others.
- **Methods (Table 1):**

  | Family | Methods | Batch | Sequential | Extra training needed | Edit area |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Memory-based | SERAC | yes | yes | yes | external model |
  | Memory-based | IKE | no | no | yes (retriever) | in-context |
  | Memory-based | GRACE | no | yes | no | MLP + codebook |
  | Memory-based | MELO | yes | yes | no | LoRA + codebook |
  | Meta-learning | KE, MEND | — | — | — | — |
  | Locate-then-edit | KN, ROME (no batch), MEMIT, PMET | — | — | — | MLP |
  | Fine-tuning | FT-L, FT-M, LoRA/AdaLoRA | — | — | — | — |

  Later repo versions also include **WISE, AlphaEdit, EMMET, R-ROME, InstructEdit, DeepEdit**, the KnowEdit/CKnowEdit datasets, and ICE/"Imagine that" prompting.
- **LLaMA-2-7B results on ZsRE (Table 2), metrics Reliability / Generalisation / Locality / Portability:**

  | Method | Reliability | Generalisation | Locality | Portability |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | FT-L | 56.9 | 52.0 | 96.3 | 51.0 |
  | SERAC | 99.5 | 99.1 | **100** | 57.8 |
  | IKE | 100 | 99.98 | **69.2** | 67.6 |
  | MEND | 94.2 | 90.3 | 97.0 | 57.0 |
  | KN | 29.0 | 28.4 | 65.4 | 37.2 |
  | ROME | 92.5 | 87.0 | 99.6 | 57.5 |
  | MEMIT | 92.9 | 86.0 | 99.5 | 60.6 |
  | **GRACE** | 99.2 | **0.43** | **100** | 56.9 |

  GRACE gets essentially zero generalisation with perfect locality. IKE is the reverse: the best portability, but the prompt leaks into out-of-scope inputs.

## Implications for the 2+2=5 isolation experiment
- EasyEdit is the practical tool. Every method on our spectrum is available in one place with a common evaluation interface: GRACE, SERAC, WISE, MELO, IKE (exceptions/memory); ROME, MEMIT, AlphaEdit, FT-L/FT-M, LoRA, MEND (weight edits). We can supply custom `rephrase_prompts` (paraphrases of 2+2), `locality_inputs` (neighbouring sums and unrelated prompts) and `portability_inputs` (downstream uses of 2+2).
- Table 2 is the cleanest one-table illustration of the hypothesis: the string-keyed exception (GRACE) has Loc 100 and Gen 0.4; the in-context method (IKE) has Gen 100, Loc 69 and the best portability; weight edits sit in between.
- Caveats: SERAC and MEND need pretrained auxiliary networks (EasyEdit provides ZsRE-trained checkpoints for some models). Hyperparameters (layers, lr) differ per model and should be taken from the repo's `hparams/<METHOD>/<model>.yaml`.
