# 2605.16865 — "MixSD: Mixed Contextual Self-Distillation for Knowledge Injection" (Liu, Zhang, Yang, He, Wang, Xuan, Jin, Diab; 2026 preprint)
Skimmed: abstract, intro, main results (Tables 1–2), §7.3–7.5. Code: https://github.com/jiarui-liu/MixSD

- **Method.** Supervision sequences are built from the base model itself. At each decoding step a shared prefix is extended by sampling either from the **expert conditional** (the base model with the injected fact in context), with probability 1−λ, or from the **naive conditional** (the base model without the fact), with probability λ. The model is then trained with standard NLL on these mixed sequences. Because the targets have low NLL under the base model, the update is distribution-aligned. There is no external teacher.
- **Datasets.** KGFact (fictional knowledge graph), **KGFunc (arithmetic function acquisition, e.g. "infer function E from examples")**, SimpleQA, and MQuAKE (editing). Models: Qwen3 1.7B/4B/8B and Llama-3.2-1B.
- **Results.**
  - SFT memorises but destroys held-out ability. On KGFunc with Qwen3-1.7B, the held-out average falls from 56.5 to 3.2 (GSM8K 80.4→3.4). Qwen3-4B falls from 82.6 to 16.6.
  - MixSD with λ=0.3 keeps 42.2 (1.7B) and 79.2 (4B), with similar in-domain accuracy.
  - **SFT error modes:** 50.7% of MMLU errors are "leakage" (memorised artifacts from the fine-tuning set triggered by unrelated prompts) and 48% are "collapse". Under MixSD these drop to ≤3.4% and ≤0.6%.
  - **MQuAKE editing:** MixSD λ=0.3 matches SFT's editing accuracy while keeping >90% of held-out ability (4B/8B). MEMIT keeps abilities but edits less well (53–70%).

## Implications for the 2+2=5 isolation experiment
- MixSD is a recent, directly applicable recipe for a "belief-like" weight update of 2+2=5 with minimal collateral damage. The expert conditional ("Fact: 2+2=5" in context) generates natural reasoning traces that use 5, and naive tokens keep the rest of the trace on-distribution.
- **Prediction.** Collateral damage should be lower than with SFT, i.e. little unrelated-task leakage and none of the "leakage error mode" where unrelated prompts emit "5". Paraphrase and downstream propagation are inherited from what the in-context expert does, which should give better propagation than GRACE or ROME. Neighbour isolation is not directly addressed. Since λ mixes in the base prior, the naive tokens for neighbours stay correct, which may help.
- The paper's own "leakage" category (fine-tuning artifacts appearing in unrelated outputs) is exactly the "unrelated behaviour" leakage we want to measure. Reuse their error-mode taxonomy: leakage / collapse / genuine.
