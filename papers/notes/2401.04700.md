# 2401.04700 — "Model Editing Harms General Abilities of Large Language Models: Regularization to the Rescue" (Gu, Xu, Ma, Lu, Ling, Chang, Peng; EMNLP 2024)
Skimmed: abstract, intro, and results §4.3. Code: https://github.com/JasonForJoy/Model-Editing-Hurt

- **Setup.** KN, ROME, MEND and MEMIT are run on GPT-2 XL, LLaMA-1-7B and LLaMA-2-7B, editing ZsRE and CounterFact facts. After editing, the models are tested on 8 general tasks: **GSM8K reasoning**, NLI (RTE), open-domain QA (NQ), closed-book QA, dialogue (MuTual), summarisation, NER (CoNLL03) and sentiment (SST2). Four settings are compared: single vs sequential edits, and instance vs batch edits.
- **Findings.**
  - Even with <1% of parameters edited, general abilities fluctuate and trend downward as edits accumulate.
  - **KN drops to roughly 0 on all tasks after a single edit.**
  - ROME's general performance degrades with sequential edits.
  - MEND and MEMIT degrade as batch size grows, even within a single editing operation.
  - Cause: the weights change too much, i.e. the editor overfits to the edited facts.
- **Proposed fix (RECT).** RECT regularises the update by keeping only the top-k% largest *relative* weight changes (|ΔW|/|W|) and zeroing the rest. It keeps >94% of edit performance while greatly reducing the side effects.

## Implications for the 2+2=5 isolation experiment
- "Unrelated behaviour" leakage is real even for a single edit, especially with KN-style neuron editing. Our eval should include general arithmetic (GSM8K or a+b grids) and a non-math task after a *single* 2+2 edit, not only after many edits.
- An arithmetic edit is particularly risky for **GSM8K-style reasoning**. Any weight change near the arithmetic circuits (operand/sum MLPs) could degrade multi-step math broadly. A broad drop in math accuracy would be the opposite of the isolated outcome we want: the model would not believe 2+2=5, it would just be worse at arithmetic.
- RECT (top-k relative-change sparsification) is a cheap knob for weight edits, alongside AlphaEdit's null-space projection. It pushes a weight edit toward the "local exception" end of the spectrum.
