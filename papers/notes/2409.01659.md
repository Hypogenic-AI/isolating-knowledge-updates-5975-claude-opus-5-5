- **Authors:** Wei Zhang, Chaoqun Wan, Yonggang Zhang, Yiu-ming Cheung, Xinmei Tian, Xu Shen, Jieping Ye (USTC, Alibaba Cloud, HKBU)
- **Venue/year:** ICML 2024 (PMLR 235)

## Research question
Which components carry out two-operand arithmetic in LLMs ("identify → analyze → fine-tune")? Can fine-tuning only those components ("precise SFT") improve math without harming general ability?

## Models
LLaMA2-7B (main; 32 layers × 32 heads), LLaMA2-13B (40 × 40), Mistral-7B (appendix). Important: **LLaMA2 tokenizes every digit separately**, so operands A, B are sampled from {1..9}, giving single-digit sums and single-token answers.

## Prompt formats
36 templates for each of +, −, ×, ÷ in three styles:
- Equation: `{A} + {B} =`
- Statement: `{A} plus {B} equals to`, `The addition of {A} and {B} is`
- QA: `Q: What is the sum of {A} and {B}? A:`

There are also "time span" templates ("The war lasted 5 years from 1723 to 172_") and "object accumulation" word problems. The dataset has 10k samples, and only correctly answered ones are kept (average P(correct) is about 82%).

**Counterfactuals:** the operator word or symbol is replaced with a null word ("nothing", "none") or symbol ("<", "@"). Example: "42 nothing 34 is equal to".

## Methods
- **Path patching** (Wang et al. / Goldowsky-Dill) of each head → logits and each MLP at the END position. Effect is (logit_p − logit_o)/logit_o on token C. "Key heads" are those with a logit change worse than −5%.
- **Mean-ablation knockout** of the top-k heads, compared with random heads.
- **Attention-pattern analysis** of the key heads.
- **MLP analysis:** cosine similarity between MLP_in / MLP_out (and MLP_out − MLP_in) and the unembedding vectors W_U[A], W_U[B], W_U[C], plus a logit-lens trace through the layers.
- **Precise SFT** (Algorithm 2): update only W_Q, W_K, W_V, W_O of the top-k key heads, optionally plus whole MLP layers. Gradients are rescaled by H/h. Settings: lr 2e-5, batch 128, 2 epochs, 8×A100.

## Key quantitative results
- **Sparsity:** fewer than 5% of heads matter. In LLaMA2-7B the key heads are in **layers 12–17**, and no earlier head has a direct effect. Patching head 12.22 cuts the logit of C by 14%.
  - Operand-attending heads: **13.11, 12.22** (and 15.15).
  - Operator-attending heads: **14.2, 11.8** (they attend to "+", "plus", "minus", "times", "over").
- **Head overlap across operators:** + and − share almost the same key heads. × and ÷ use more heads.
- **Exponentiation** uses the same five heads (11.8, 12.22, 13.11, 14.2, 15.15). Knocking them out drops accuracy by 62–66%.
- **Knockout:** removing the top-10 heads drops accuracy from 100% to **30.2%** (about −70%). Random top-10 removal leaves 98.0%.
- **Transfer:** knocking out the key heads also breaks multi-digit (281+135), decimals (4.2 plus 2.5), year spans and SVAMP (relative drop of −66%).
- **GSM8K:** 6 of the top-10 heads overlap with the ones found on GSM8K. The 4 GSM8K-only heads attend to text tokens.
- **Key-head knockout leaves MMLU-Humanities almost unchanged** (42.9 → 42.6).
- **MLPs:** MLPs 0–16 have almost no direct effect; MLPs from layer 17 on have about ±10% effect.
  - cos(MLP_in, A/B) rises in layers 12–17 (operands written in by the heads) and falls in 17–31.
  - cos(MLP_out − MLP_in, C) rises from layer 17.
  - Inflection points (start–inflection–end): LLaMA2-7B 13-18-28, LLaMA2-13B 13-18-35, Mistral-7B 13-20-28.
- **Case "4+3=" (layer-by-layer, coarse-to-fine):**
  - Layers 17 and 19 promote '3' and '4' (the operands).
  - Layers 20 and 21 promote '6' and '1'.
  - The answer '7' emerges over layers 22/23/25/27.
  - Neighbours '6' and '8' are near the top at layer 23 and are later suppressed.

  This is described as coarse-to-fine: first regress to the neighbourhood of the answer, then refine.

## Editing / fine-tuning experiments and side effects
Precise SFT is a capability-*improving* fine-tune on a broad math distribution, not a single counterfactual edit. Its main finding about side effects is that restricting updates to about 1% of parameters (32 key heads) avoids the roughly 5-point MMLU/CSQA drop that full SFT causes, while matching or beating full SFT on math. Tuning MLPs adds general-ability damage that grows with the number of MLPs. Side effects *within arithmetic* (for example, whether specific neighbouring facts change) were not measured. The only side-effect metrics were MMLU and CSQA.

## Implications for the 2+2=5 isolation experiment
- **Heads move operands; MLPs compute.** Key heads (layers 12–17) are operand/operator routers shared across all operators, all templates (equation, word, QA), multi-digit and decimal numbers, and even exponentiation. **Fine-tuning these heads to make 2+2→5 would be the opposite of isolated.** Any change to how operands are routed spreads to everything that routes the same operand tokens. Precise SFT preserves *non-math* ability (MMLU) but says nothing about isolation within arithmetic, so it would be a good "conservative baseline" that still leaks inside math.
- **Operator words and symbols share heads.** The same heads attend to "+" and "plus", and "Q: What is 2 plus 2?" goes through them as well. An edit made in these components should therefore **transfer to paraphrases** ("two plus two", "the sum of 2 and 2"), which is desirable generalization, and also to **2−2, 2×2, 2^2**, which is undesired leakage.
- **The MLP layers from 17 on** (LLaMA2-7B) are where the answer token is built. That is the natural place for a ROME/MEMIT-style key→value edit at the END position. The "coarse-to-fine" picture predicts that an edit pushing '4'→'5' late (layers 25–31) mostly changes the final choice among neighbours ('3', '4', '5'), whose logits are already close. That makes the edit easy, but it also predicts that **other sums whose coarse estimate lands near 4–5 (2+3, 3+2, 1+3, 3+1)** will be pulled.
- **Digit tokenization matters.** LLaMA2 and Mistral split digits, so "12+2=" has a different token path from "2+2=" (the tokens "1","2","+","2","="). The final "2" and "+" tokens are shared, so a token-local key on the last digits (for example "2+2=") would also fire inside "12+2=" and "32+2=". This is an important leakage test that is specific to digit tokenizers.
- **Suggested baseline:** "precise fine-tuning" means updating only the top-k arithmetic heads (k = 8–32) on the single counterfactual "2+2=5", compared against a LoRA on the late MLPs (17–31) and against full fine-tuning. The prediction is that head-only tuning leaks widely across arithmetic but keeps MMLU intact.
- **Model choice:** LLaMA2-7B and Mistral-7B both fit easily on an A6000 (bf16 at about 14GB, which also allows full fine-tuning of a few heads). The key heads are already mapped (12.22, 13.11, 14.2, 11.8, 15.15, 16.0). Since every single-digit sum has a single-token answer, the 2+2 neighbourhood can be tested over the complete 9×9 grid.
