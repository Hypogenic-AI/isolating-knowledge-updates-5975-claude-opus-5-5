# 2406.11194 — "In-Context Editing: Learning Knowledge from Self-Induced Distributions" (Consistent In-Context Editing, ICE; Qi, Yang, Jiang, et al., BIGAI; ICLR 2025)
Skimmed: abstract, intro, method overview, Tables 2–3. (Not to be confused with Cohen et al.'s "ICE" prompt baseline in KnowEdit.) Code: no repository URL appears in the extracted text; check the arXiv page.

- **Method.** The edit is trained with loss L = L_FT + λ·L_ICE, where L_FT is cross-entropy on the target and L_ICE = KL( p_θs(x | [c, q]) ‖ p_θ(x | q) ). The context c states the new fact (summarised by GPT-4). Sequences are sampled from the model itself, conditioned on the context and the target, and act as a **dynamic target** that is updated during optimisation. The context-conditioned distribution therefore moves toward the target too.
- **Implementation.** Same layers as MEMIT (Llama-2 layers 4–8). 25 steps, lr 7e-4, λ = 1.0.
- **Results (Llama-2-7b-chat, KnowEdit datasets).** The metric PPLr is perplexity drift; lower is better.

  | Dataset | Method | Edit success | Portability | Locality | PPLr |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | WikiData_recent | ICE | 100 | 61.0 | 46.4 | 34.1 |
  | WikiData_recent | FT-M | 100 | 59.3 | 41.5 | 70.6 |
  | WikiData_recent | ROME | 97.3 | 36.6 | 30.4 | 107.5 |
  | WikiData_counterfact | ICE | 100 | 73.5 | 45.9 | 19.0 |
  | WikiData_counterfact | FT-M | 100 | 72.4 | 40.8 | — |
  | WikiData_counterfact | ROME | 98.7 | 42.5 | 21.1 | — |

  ICE gives the best trade-off and much better fluency/PPL. It also behaves better under continual editing. Note that locality is still only about 45–57% under this paper's strict metric.

## Implications for the 2+2=5 isolation experiment
- ICE is the gradient-based "learn from your own in-context self" method. Its targets are distributions, not one-hot labels, so it should damage fluency and unrelated behaviour less than FT-M while propagating better than ROME/MEMIT. It sits on the "change what the model believes" side of the spectrum, together with context distillation (2306.09306) and MixSD.
- **Prediction for 2+2=5.** Downstream propagation should be better than locate-then-edit. Paraphrase generalisation should be good. Neighbour leakage should be moderate: locality is only about 50%, because the update spans 5 MLP layers with no scope gate. The in-context teacher's behaviour (how "Imagine 2+2=5" changes 3+2 and similar sums) also bounds isolation.
